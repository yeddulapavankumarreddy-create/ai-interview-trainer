HR Interview Questions and Best Practices
==========================================

COMMON HR INTERVIEW QUESTIONS
-------------------------------
Q: Tell me about yourself.
A: Structure: brief professional background (1-2 sentences), relevant skills and experience (2-3 sentences), why you are excited about this role (1 sentence). Keep to 90 seconds. Focus on professional, not personal. End with enthusiasm for the opportunity.

Q: Why are you interested in this role?
A: Research the company and role beforehand. Connect your skills and interests to specific role requirements. Mention company values, products, or mission that resonates. Show you have done your homework.

Q: What are your greatest strengths?
A: Choose strengths relevant to the role. Back each with a specific example. Avoid generic answers like "hard worker." Good examples: "I am skilled at breaking down complex problems — for instance, in my project I..."

Q: What is your greatest weakness?
A: Choose a real weakness but one that is not critical to the role. Show you are working to improve it. Example: "I used to struggle with delegating tasks, but I have been actively practising this by..."

Q: Where do you see yourself in five years?
A: Show ambition aligned with realistic growth in the company. Research the role's career path. Focus on skills you want to develop and contributions you want to make. Avoid saying you want the interviewer's job.

Q: Why should we hire you?
A: Summarise your top 3 relevant strengths and how they match the role requirements. Provide brief evidence. End with enthusiasm about contributing to the team.

Q: What do you know about our company?
A: Research: company mission, products/services, recent news, culture values, major customers, key achievements. Mention 2-3 specific things that impressed you or connect to your values.

Q: What are your salary expectations?
A: Research market rates beforehand (Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary). Provide a range based on experience and role level. It's acceptable to say you are flexible and want to understand the full package.

Q: Why are you leaving your current job?
A: Keep positive. Focus on growth opportunities, new challenges, or better role fit. Never criticise current employer. Example: "I'm looking for a role where I can..."

QUESTIONS TO ASK THE INTERVIEWER
----------------------------------
- What does a typical day look like for this role?
- What are the biggest challenges the team is currently facing?
- How do you measure success in this role?
- What opportunities exist for professional development?
- What is the team culture like?
- What are the next steps in the hiring process?

BODY LANGUAGE AND COMMUNICATION TIPS
--------------------------------------
- Maintain appropriate eye contact
- Sit upright, lean slightly forward to show engagement
- Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
- Avoid filler words (um, uh, like, you know)
- Use the candidate's name occasionally
- Ask for clarification if a question is unclear — better than answering the wrong question
- Match the interviewer's energy and formality level

PREPARATION CHECKLIST
-----------------------
Before the interview:
- Research the company thoroughly
- Re-read the job description
- Prepare 5-7 STAR stories from your experience
- Practise answers aloud
- Prepare 3-5 questions to ask
- Choose professional attire
- Plan your route/tech setup
- Bring copies of your resume (in-person)

During the interview:
- Arrive or log in 5 minutes early
- Greet warmly and professionally
- Listen carefully to each question
- Take a moment to think before answering
- Provide specific examples, not generalities
- Show genuine enthusiasm

After the interview:
- Send a thank-you email within 24 hours
- Reference a specific topic from the interview
- Express continued interest in the role
